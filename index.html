MevzuatRadar

Open source

Regulatory change intelligence for Turkish banking law.

Rules from BDDK, MASAK and TCMB change often, and the amendment texts are hard to read on their own: one sentence can change a heading and three different paragraphs at once. MevzuatRadar finds those amendment acts in the Official Gazette, extracts structured change records (operation, target article, paragraph and clause, old and new wording) and verifies them against the consolidated law text, with no manually labelled data.

What I built

  • End-to-end pipeline: collection, discovery of amendment acts, parsing, extraction and verification
  • Label-free verification: if a change was extracted correctly, its effect must appear in the published current text
  • Turned verified output into silver-labelled training data, fine-tuned mT5-small and benchmarked it against few-shot Qwen2.5-7B through the same evaluation pipeline
  • FastAPI service with Docker for extraction, verification and point-in-time queries, such as what an article said in 2019
  • A daily Official Gazette watcher on GitHub Actions, with 139 automated tests running in CI on every push

Results

  • 98.3% verification rate across 13 regulations, 84 amendment acts and 460 records
  • 97.5% on two regulations held out as an untouched test set
  • Rules 97.5%, fine-tuned small model 80.5%, LLM 77.8%; a rules and model hybrid recovered 5% more verified changes

Uzlaştırma Belge Oluşturucu

Legal document generator

A web app that turns one form into a criminal mediator's entire paperwork.

Criminal mediators (uzlaştırmacı) prepare the same documents over and over for every case. The user fills in the case, mediator and party details once and the app generates the paperwork.

What I built

  • 12 document types from a single form: invitation letters, settlement offers and mediation reports
  • Export to Word, PDF and UDF, the format used by Turkey's UYAP court system
  • Support for multiple parties per case, with a preview before anything is generated
  • Privacy by design: personal data is never stored, documents are built in memory only
