Frequently asked

  • Is my progress saved? Yes. Your level, score, stars and settings are saved on your device automatically.
  • Does the game need the internet? No. Splash Fairies works fully offline.
  • Are there ads or in-app purchases? No.
  • How do I turn off sound or music? Use the sound and music buttons on the main menu or in the pause menu.

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 24, 2026

Splash Fairies is made by İlayda Gürocak. Your privacy matters, so the game is built to work without collecting anything about you.

Information we collect

None. Splash Fairies does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal information. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking of any kind.

Information stored on your device

To remember your progress, the game saves your level, score, stars, collected water, magic needles and sound settings on your device using standard iOS storage. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us. Deleting the app removes it.

Third-party services

The game does not include any third-party SDKs, analytics tools or ad networks, and it does not connect to the internet.

Children

Because the game collects no personal information, it is suitable for players of all ages. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
