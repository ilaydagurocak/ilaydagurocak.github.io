    About

    I'm a computer engineer in İstanbul who likes owning a product from the first question to the last metric.

    I started in native mobile: SwiftUI and Kotlin apps, Bluetooth links to IoT devices, Firebase and Node.js backends, and an App Store release that I later transferred to a client. Then I moved into applied NLP and LLM work, where I care most about evaluation: a model's output is only useful once you can show how often it is right.

    That's why my projects end in numbers. MevzuatRadar checks every extracted legal change against the published law. Player Feedback Radar ties each kind of player complaint to a KPI a product team can move. I want to keep doing exactly that — turning what users say into product decisions.
